# Executive-Level Aiceberg Demo Script (5-7 minutes)

## **Opening (30 seconds)**
*"We integrated Aiceberg's AI monitoring into your existing OpenWebUI system. Let me show you what complete AI visibility looks like."*

## **The Demo (3-4 minutes)**

### **Step 1: Show the Problem**
*"Right now, when your employees use AI tools, you have zero visibility. Let me show you..."*

- Open regular OpenWebUI
- Ask: "What's our company's revenue strategy?"
- *"This conversation just happened in a black box. You don't know what was asked or answered."*

### **Step 2: Show the Solution**
*"Now, here's the same system with Aiceberg monitoring..."*

- Switch to monitored OpenWebUI
- Ask the same question
- Show the Aiceberg dashboard updating in real-time
- *"Every question and every AI response is now visible and logged."*

### **Step 3: Show Protection**
*"And here's what happens when someone asks something inappropriate..."*

- Ask something that triggers blocking
- Show the user gets a policy message instead of an AI answer
- *"The AI never sees the problematic content. It's blocked before it gets there."*

## **Business Impact (2 minutes)**

### **What This Solves:**
- **Compliance**: "Every AI conversation is now audit-ready"
- **Data Protection**: "Sensitive information can't leak to AI companies"  
- **Risk Management**: "Inappropriate AI responses are blocked automatically"

### **The Bottom Line:**
*"This took us 15 minutes to set up. Your employees use AI exactly the same way they always have. But now you can see everything and protect everything."*

## **Close (30 seconds)**
*"The question isn't whether your employees are using AI - they already are. The question is: do you want to see what they're doing, or stay in the dark?"*

## **Key Phrases for Executives:**

### **Risk-Focused:**
- *"Every AI conversation that happens without monitoring is a potential compliance violation"*
- *"This prevents the data breach headlines you don't want to be in"*
- *"Your insurance company will want to know you have this protection"*

### **Business-Focused:**
- *"Zero disruption to employee workflows"*
- *"Complete audit trail for any regulator"*  
- *"Protects your competitive information from leaking"*

### **Simple Technology:**
- *"It sits between your employees and the AI"*
- *"Think of it as a security camera for AI conversations"*
- *"Same AI tools, same user experience, but now with visibility"*

## **If They Ask Technical Questions:**

**"How hard is this to deploy?"**
*"Our IT team had it running in under an hour."*

**"Does it slow anything down?"**
*"No - the monitoring happens in parallel. Users see the same response times."*

**"What about privacy?"**
*"You control exactly what gets monitored. Some clients only monitor certain departments."*

**"How much does it cost?"**
*"Less than what you'd pay for a single compliance fine. We can discuss exact pricing after you see if this fits your needs."*

## **Demo Prep Checklist:**
- [ ] Have both environments ready (monitored vs unmonitored)
- [ ] Test your blocking trigger phrase
- [ ] Have Aiceberg dashboard open in another browser tab
- [ ] Keep it simple - no technical jargon
- [ ] Focus on business outcomes, not how it works

**Remember: Executives care about risk, compliance, and business impact - not technical implementation.**
